# Provenance — AegisOS build `BUILD_NUMBER=2026082417`

## Relationship to the pinned upstream revision

This is **not** a new upstream sync. It is an AegisOS build produced from
the same source tree recorded in `2026071500.md` (manifest tag
`2026071500`, `platform-primary` on the build server), rebuilt with
`OFFICIAL_BUILD=true` exported. No `repo sync` occurred between the
original sync and this build.

## Why this build exists

`build/make/target/product/media_system.mk` gates GrapheneOS's real
Updater app behind `ifeq ($(OFFICIAL_BUILD),true)`. Every prior AegisOS
`lynx` build (the 2026-08-04 alpha candidate, the Aug-16 duress dummy-cost
build, and this rebuild's own first attempt) had `OFFICIAL_BUILD` unset and
silently shipped without the Updater app — no build warning, clean exit
code. Root-caused and fixed in ADR-047
(`aegisos-foundation/docs/governance/decision-log.md`); durable tooling
detail in `aegisos-foundation/docs/research/build-and-signing-engineering-notes.md`.

## Known gap

This is the only retroactive provenance record added for a post-sync
AegisOS build revision. Earlier build numbers that shipped in the interim
(e.g. the 2026-07-30 emulator build `2026073000`, the 2026-08-04 accepted
alpha candidate, the Aug-16 duress dummy-cost primitive build) are **not**
individually recorded here — their evidence exists only scattered across
decision-log ADRs and build-server evidence paths, not consolidated as
`aegisos-manifest` provenance records. Backfilling those was out of scope
for this pass; treat their absence here as a known gap, not confirmation
they didn't happen.
